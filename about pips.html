Welcome to PIPS College

PIPS college is a premier institution of higher learning located in Abbotabad, Pakistam. We are committed to providing high-quality education to our students, empowering them to succeed in their chosen careers and make a positive impact in the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a supportive and inclusive learning environment that fosters academic excellence, creativity, and critical thinking. We aim to inspire our students to become lifelong learners, equipped with the skills, knowledge, and values necessary to succeed in an ever-changing world.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a leading institution of higher learning, recognized for our academic excellence, innovative research, and commitment to social responsibility. We aim to create a community of scholars who are passionate about learning, dedicated to excellence, and driven to make a positive difference in the world.

Our History

PIPS was established in 2003 , with the goal of providing high-quality education to students from all backgrounds. Over the years, we have grown and evolved, expanding our academic programs, facilities, and services to meet the changing needs of our students and the community.

Our Values

  • Academic excellence and rigor
  • Creativity, innovation, and entrepreneurship
  • Diversity, equity, and inclusion
  • Social responsibility and community engagement
  • Integrity, honesty, and transparency
